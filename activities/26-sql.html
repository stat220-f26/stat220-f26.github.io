```{sql}
--| connection: db
SELECT * FROM kind_type LIMIT 0,3;
```

COUNT and GROUP BY

COUNT(*) counts rows, and GROUP BY gives one row per group (like group_by() + summarize()):

dbGetQuery(db,
"SELECT production_year, COUNT(*) AS n_movies
 FROM title
 WHERE kind_id = 1 AND production_year BETWEEN 2000 AND 2020
 GROUP BY production_year
 ORDER BY n_movies DESC
 LIMIT 0,5;")

(The busiest year in this range is 2016, with 59,702 movies.) Every column in SELECT that is not a summary must also be in GROUP BY.
